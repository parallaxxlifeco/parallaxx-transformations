## 10. Memberships

These terms apply to The Reconnected Woman and The Reconnected Man memberships.

**Billing.** Membership is a monthly subscription, paid through Stripe. It renews automatically each month on the date you joined, at the price you joined at.

**Your price.** Your price stays the same for as long as your membership continues. If the price for new members goes up, yours doesn't.

**Cancelling.** You can cancel at any time by letting Daniel know. Your cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you've already paid for, and you keep access to sessions until then. No further payments are taken.

**Refunds.** Payments for a month that has already started aren't refunded, except where the law requires it.

**Pausing.** You can pause your membership once in any 12-month period, for up to one month, by asking before your next renewal date. No payment is taken during the pause, you keep your price, and billing resumes on your usual renewal date.

**Rejoining.** If you cancel, you're welcome to rejoin after six months, at the price for new members at that time.

**Sessions and themes.** Sessions run live online. Each month follows a theme, and themes aren't repeated, so sessions you miss aren't made up later. Session times and themes may change, and we'll give notice when they do.

**Confidentiality.** Members keep what's shared in sessions confidential. Sessions may be recorded for internal use. Daniel may draw on themes and lines from sessions in his articles and content, without your name or any detail that could identify you. Anything that identifies you, such as a clip, your name, image or voice, or a testimonial, is only used with your personal consent first.
