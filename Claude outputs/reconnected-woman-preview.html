Common Questions

Before You Come

Isn't this just another women's circle?
No. Most are built around becoming more feminine, and there's a performance in that. Here we call it as it is, so you can put something down instead of picking more up.
Why is a man hosting it?
Because the women who join want a healthy masculine presence in their life, one they are not being led by, competing with, or taking instruction from. Just the masculine holding the room, so they can stop for a change.
I have great friends. Do I need this?
Friendship runs on co-regulation and the women you'd go to are running your operating system. I'm not here to be your friend.
Can I come once before I decide?
Yes. The first session of every month is open and there's no charge for it. Come, sit in it, and decide from the room rather than from this page. After that it's €89 a month, and you're grandfathered: your price never increases as long as you stay.
Where does it run?
Online, so you can join from wherever you are. Nothing to travel to and nothing to arrange around it.
Do I need to attend every session?
No. Life moves and the group knows it. Come when you can, and know that the women who turn up regularly are the ones who feel it shift.
When do sessions run?
One sitting time, set so it works across the time zones the group is spread over, and we stay fluid with what the group asks for. Come to the open session and you'll see how it sits with your week.
What does the fee include?
Facilitation, hot seat work, resources, and the people you'll meet. It's largely a filter.
Are there upsells coming?
No. There's the 1:1 Reconnect Program if you decide you want more, faster, deeper, and your membership investment is discounted from that. Same work, same direction. More personal.
