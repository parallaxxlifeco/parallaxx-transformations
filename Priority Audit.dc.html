Before your result

Our approach to life is built on three pillars.

Needs

What you require to operate at your potential. Met with consistency, care and respect.

Boundaries

What you accept, and what you are able to decline. Honoured, including by you.

Emotions

What you feel, as an internal compass that highlights your needs and your boundaries.

These are not three skills to go and develop. They are three dimensions of self that were supposed to be met for you, consistently, by somebody else, a long time ago. Most of us got some of them. Whatever was missing, you found another way to fulfil, and in your case that way has worked extremely well.

All three hold each other up. We are only as equipped as the weakest one.

Your three

Your three foundational pillars of self.

Each bar shows how far that pillar is from being met in the way that would support you best. The lower the score, the more developed that pillar already is. A higher score means less practice and less focus have gone into protecting and developing that part of yourself.

What is underneath all three

No single response to any of the fifteen statements means much on its own, which is why this is hard to see. Combined, they reveal a pattern, and the pattern gives you clues about the outcomes in your life.

The three pillars together are what fuel our self-esteem. Left underdeveloped, they leave self-esteem as something that happens to you, depending on how the quarter went. It can go the other way too. Developing yourself through the three pillars you just scored stops life outcomes being a matter of chance and makes them a matter of intentional design.

Where this sits

Reconnect runs on three dimensions. Vision, how you would like things to be. Values, who you need to become to live that vision. Velocity, how you move the pieces into place to make it happen. What you just scored sits inside values, and these are changes you can start making right away.

Needs, boundaries and emotions are not three problems to fix. They are the three dimensions every one of these conversations turns out to be about, and most people are not giving them enough attention, with intention.

What they add up to is being able to handle the ambiguity and the mistakes and not having it all together, without any of it touching what you think you are worth. Worth that does not need to be won, and is not measured by how much you do, because what you do is built on real self-esteem.

That is the work. And it is not left to chance.
