IMPORTANT — why this list is shorter than a lookup suggests
----------------------------------------------------------
A normal DNS lookup chases CNAMEs. Ask for the MX of mail.parallaxxtransformations.com
and the resolver follows the CNAME to privateemail.com and returns THAT domain's
MX records as if they were yours. Same for the A record and SPF under vault
(Teachable's) and members (LeadConnector's).

A CNAME cannot coexist with any other record at the same host. So recreating
those inherited entries alongside the CNAME will either be rejected or will
break the subdomain. Create the CNAME and nothing else.

SUBDOMAIN — one CNAME, nothing more
-----------------------------------
CNAME   vault               school.teachable.com

  vault = Teachable, your course platform. VERIFIED LIVE 17 Aug 2026 — it
  serves "Welcome to The Vault of Transformations!" right now. Omit this record
  and the vault goes dark at cutover.

  Do NOT also add:
    TXT vault   v=spf1 include:mailgun.org ~all     <- Teachable's, arrives via CNAME
    A   vault   34.54.192.65                        <- Teachable's current IP
  Hardcoding that IP is worse than useless: it changes without notice and you
  would be pinning the vault to an address Teachable has moved off.

  Do NOT add vault as a domain in your Vercel project. Vercel owns the apex
  and www only.

AFTER THE SWITCH — verify in this order
---------------------------------------
1. dig NS parallaxxtransformations.com          -> registrar-servers.com (Namecheap)
2. dig www.parallaxxtransformations.com         -> your Vercel CNAME target
3. dig MX parallaxxtransformations.com          -> mx1/mx2.privateemail.com
4. dig TXT default._domainkey.parallaxxtransformations.com  -> the DKIM key, intact
5. dig vault.parallaxxtransformations.com       -> school.teachable.com
   Then open it in a browser and confirm the vault actually loads.
6. Send mail IN from an outside address. Then send one OUT and check the
   headers show dkim=pass and spf=pass. This is the test that matters most,
   and the one most often skipped.
