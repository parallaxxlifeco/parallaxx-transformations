Common Questions

Using it

How long does it take each day?

As long as you want. Generally, about a ten minute check-in each morning and again in the evening.

Do I need to complete it twice a day?

No, you can complete it in the way that works for you. In fact, I personally do it once a day. Each morning I complete my reflections from the day before and then set my new day’s intentions.

Can I start on any day?

Yes. The pages aren’t dated. You write the date and tick the weekday, so day one is whenever you decide it is.

What if I miss a day?

Pick it up again the next morning. You choose your cadence. I skip weekends, for example. What tends to break your system is waiting for a ‘better week’ to start again.

I’ve never stuck with a journal. Why would this be different?

Many journals give you a blank page, putting the pressure on you to figure out where to start and what to write. Here you get the same prompts every morning and every night, which doesn’t require your own imagination.
