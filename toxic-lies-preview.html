What the book is about

Three things about time that many of us believe.

You did not make them up. They are common enough to read as common sense, which is what makes them expensive. The book names all three, and what is true instead.

Toxic lie one

“If I’m not busy, it means I’m wasting time.”

Truth

Top performers in every field prioritise rest, recovery and play.

Toxic lie two

“When I make more money, I’ll be less stressed and live more of the life I wanted.”

Truth

More money does not guarantee internal freedom.

Toxic lie three

“One more goal, one more outcome, and one day I’ll feel accomplished.”

Truth

When you know what’s important, you can ignore what’s not.
